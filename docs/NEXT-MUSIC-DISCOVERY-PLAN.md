# Planned VEXO music discovery features

Status: planning only, after the 0.24 public release. This list does not claim implementation or a delivery date.

- TikTok-inspired Trending Music
- Personalized Mixes for Your Mood
- Listening-interest learning engine
- Daily Mix and new-song discovery
- Tune My Mix controls
- Continuous playback and smart queue
- Existing VEXO UI and player integration

Preserve VEXO’s unique features, current-song continuity and manual queue priority. YouTube account connection stays optional. Trending sources, refresh cadence, catalogue matching and fallback behavior must be defined and verified before implementation.
