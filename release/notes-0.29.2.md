VEXO Music 0.29.2 fixes missing official music releases, including JANANA’s Kolama.

- Explicit song search includes provider-classified official music and lyric releases alongside audio releases.
- Artist pages load the artist’s official-recording catalogue as well as their Songs catalogue, with both sources paginated.
- Only recordings classified as music and carrying artist identity credits are accepted. Unrelated clips, podcasts and unverified uploads remain excluded.
- All recordings use VEXO’s existing audio player. Search gives closer title/artist matches priority and prefers an audio release for duplicate search results.
- Artist-page stability, following, saved music and settings are preserved.

Install over your current VEXO Music version to keep your data. Availability remains dependent on the provider and region.
