# VEXO Music 0.27.0

- Open Lyrics from Now Playing to follow timed lyrics as the song plays. Tap a line to seek.
- View untimed lyrics when available, with clear messages for instrumental tracks and unavailable lyrics.
- Artist circles scroll more smoothly and retain their position when portraits refresh.
- Lyrics are supplied by LRCLIB; song title, artist and duration are sent when lyrics are opened. Availability and timing vary by track.
- Install over your existing app to keep your music and settings.
