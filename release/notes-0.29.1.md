VEXO Music 0.29.1 fixes missing artist songs and artist-page flicker.

- Artist pages follow the selected artist’s Songs catalogue rather than a name search.
- Catalogue pagination loads more songs as you scroll. Load more remains available.
- Loading, Follow and background refreshes update the existing page, preserving artwork and scroll position.
- Recycled song rows keep larger catalogues responsive; recently opened catalogues are kept briefly in memory.
- Exact artist credits still exclude unrelated songs. Availability depends on the provider and region.

Install over your current VEXO Music app to keep music and settings.
