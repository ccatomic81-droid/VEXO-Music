# VEXO Music 0.18.0

- Made for you Home mix and personal radio use connected YouTube music, VEXO favourites, local playlists, chosen artists/genres and optional listening learning.
- Connected music also informs catalogue mix discovery, Smart discovery and cached search suggestions. User playlist order and explicit search results are preserved.
- Radio fetches additional related candidates from one diverse interest seed, with bounded requests, canonical duplicate filtering, artist variety and existing hidden-song/anti-repeat controls.
- Optional owned YouTube playlist music import: off until the user enables it. Checks at most 3 owned playlists, 50 items each, and retains at most 100 qualifying music tracks separately from the library.
- Recognizes clear music uploads outside category 10 and explains empty samples without claiming that the whole account is empty.
- Google data expires or is deleted on disconnect; pending personal discovery is cancelled when sources change. Existing playback is kept when starting radio.
