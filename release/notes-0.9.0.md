VEXO Music 0.9.0 improves the personalized Home screen and adds safe refresh, recovery and diagnostic reporting.

### What changed

- Morning greetings begin at 4 AM and follow the phone's time and time-zone changes.
- Refined Home layout, responsive music cards, improved alignment and a mini-player progress bar.
- Refresh & repair reloads temporary discovery, stream and artwork data while preserving saved music, playlists, profile and queue.
- Optional next-launch cache recovery after a recorded app crash.
- Report a problem lets users review diagnostics and open an email to ccatomic81@gmail.com. Reports are not sent silently.
- Bounded artwork loading and low-memory handling improve reliability.

Includes song suggestions while typing, personal playlists, profile photos, offline music, song radio, crossfade and built-in update checks.

### Install

Android 8.0 or newer. Package: `space.vexo.music.radio`; version code: **11**.

Install over the previous VEXO Music version to retain your data. The attached APK uses the original signing certificate; it is the upgrade APK, not a runner-signed CI test build.
