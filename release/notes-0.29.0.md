VEXO Music 0.29.0 adds a dedicated artist experience.

- Open **Library → Artists** to search for artists separately from songs.
- Follow artists to keep them in your library and Home artist circles.
- Tap an artist to open their songs and credited collaborations, matched by their catalog identity.
- Play or shuffle artist songs, and load more matches when available.
- Followed artists stay saved when song-search history is cleared.

Your notification and crossfade preferences are preserved. Android notification permission remains required for alerts. Install over your current VEXO Music app to keep your music and settings.
