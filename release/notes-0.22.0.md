# VEXO Music 0.22.0

Artwork now reloads after Refresh & repair, retries temporary failures, and falls back to the same song's public thumbnail when artwork metadata is missing or unavailable. Search, Home, the queue, the mini-player and Now Playing share the fix.

This public update also includes the simpler Home/Search interface, session-aware VEXO recommendations, manual queue priority, shuffle-safe Play next and labelled automatic recommendations from the tester releases. YouTube account connection remains optional. MindRadio, PureSong, Smart Experience, crossfade, offline music, accounts and playlists remain available.

Install as an update over your existing VEXO app to preserve data. The app ID and signing certificate remain unchanged. Android must still approve installation. Real-phone audio, background behavior and provider availability remain dependent on the device and network.
